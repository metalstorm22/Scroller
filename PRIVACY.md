# Scroller Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Scroller is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what the Chrome extension accesses and how that information is handled.

## Data transmission

Scroller does not transmit, sell, or share user data. The developer and third parties do not receive your page addresses, scroll positions, keyframes, preferences, page content, personal information, authentication information, financial information, health information, or location.

Scroller does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, remote code, or third-party services.

## Information handled on your device

Scroller processes the current page's origin and path and your scroll position to provide its user-facing keyframe features. It uses Chrome's extension storage to save that origin and path, the scroll keyframes and timing values you create, and interface preferences. Timelines are keyed to the current page origin and path so they can be restored when you return. A temporary session value may also be stored for up to 30 seconds when you choose **Reload & play**, allowing playback to resume after the page reloads.

Scroller does not read, copy, or save the content of the page. The locally stored page address and keyframe information remains in your Chrome browser and is not sent to the developer or any third party. You can remove a timeline through Scroller's **Clear all keyframes** action. Uninstalling the extension removes its extension storage.

## Permissions

Scroller uses these Chrome permissions:

- `activeTab` grants temporary access to the current tab after you explicitly select the extension or use one of its keyboard shortcuts.
- `scripting` inserts Scroller's local interface and playback code into that active tab.
- `storage` saves your timelines and preferences locally and supports the short-lived **Reload & play** handoff.

Scroller does not request broad host permissions and does not run automatically on every website.

## Limited use

Scroller uses information received from Chrome APIs only to provide and improve its disclosed single purpose: designing and replaying timed webpage scrolling sequences. Its use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes to this policy

If Scroller's data practices change, this policy will be updated before a release containing those changes is published. The date at the top will reflect the latest revision.
